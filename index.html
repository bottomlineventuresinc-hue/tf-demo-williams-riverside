Asked every week

Straight answers about mess, timing, and money.

Will there be mess?

Yes. Indoor plumbing routes get floor protection, outdoor digs stay marked, and there is a sweep-down at the end of every working day.

Will it run over?

Most jobs finish on the written schedule. Hidden leaks, utility locates, and parts delays are the honest exceptions. You hear from your site lead within 24 hours with the revised date in writing.

How do payments work?

Staged in the contract: 10% to book, 30% on start, 30% at mid-point, 25% at finish work, and the final 5% only after the punch list is cleared at sign-off.

Do we have to move out?

Usually not. Plumbing and sprinkler work is staged so water stays available to the rest of the house when the layout allows.
